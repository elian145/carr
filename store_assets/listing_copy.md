## Short description (≤80 chars)

Buy and sell cars near you — listings, chat, and dealers in one app.

## Full description

CarNet is a car marketplace for buying and selling vehicles.

• Browse listings with photos, specs, and filters  
• Message sellers in-app chat  
• Create and manage your own listings  
• Find dealerships and dealer profiles  
• Save favorites and compare cars  
• English, Arabic, and Kurdish  

Deals are arranged off-platform — CarNet does not process payments in the app.
