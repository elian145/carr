Released in August 1997, the second-generation Aristo inherited the general appearance of its predecessor, including the basic proportions with its long nose and short deck. The styling was executed by an in-house design team. With the adoption of a newly developed platform, the front and rear overhangs were shortened and the engine was moved back, optimizing the weight distribution and significantly improving maneuverability. The specifications of the 3-liter inline 6-cylinder DOHC 24-valve engine remained basically unchanged from its predecessor's, including the choice of naturally aspirated or twin turbocharged versions, although the adoption of VVT-i and other improvements in the intake system boosted the output. Torque-regulating electronic devices, including the vehicle stability control (VSC) and traction control systems, were also incorporated. After the second generation, the Aristo line was discontinued to be succeeded by the Lexus GS.
