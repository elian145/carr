PLUG-IN HYBRID

## Roam on with flexible fueling.

Prius Prime is more than just a hybrid — it’s a hybrid you can charge. Thanks to its larger battery pack, a full charge has an EPA-estimated all-electric driving range of up to 44 miles. 1 So plug it in at home, at work or most public charging stations nearby to help maximize fuel efficiency. And if you can’t find a charge, it’ll run on its hybrid engine to continue your drives.

##### Intelligent efficiency

The gas engine and electric motors work together to provide sufficient power when you need it and remarkable efficiency when you don’t.

##### Impressive performance

Prius Prime’s Hybrid Synergy Drive® system helps maximize output from both the gas engine and electric motors. With 220 net combined horsepower, it has plenty of power and gets off the line quickly, with a 0-60 mph time of 6.6 seconds. 2

##### On-road confidence

With an impressive 10-year or 150,000-mile hybrid system warranty, 3 Prius Prime’s battery is designed to last. Along with ToyotaCare 4 that covers normal factory scheduled service for the first 2 years or 25,000 miles, whichever comes first, and 3-year, unlimited-mileage 24-hour Roadside Assistance 4 to help keep you going, there may be local savings, incentives 5 and HOV eligibility 6 to make the decision to drive Prius Prime even easier.

##### Traffic Jam Assist

Enjoy city driving with newfound ease. Traffic Jam Assist (TJA) 10 helps navigate the ebb and flow of stop-and-go traffic on limited access roadways at speeds under 25 mph, by providing hands-free control of the vehicle’s steering, braking and acceleration.

##### Top-mount Multi-Information Display

Information that meets the eye. Prius Prime’s top-mount Multi-Information Display (MID) is placed right above the steering wheel to clearly give you vehicle details, outside temperature, driver-assist system information and more—all within easy view.

##### Panoramic View Monitor

Get a better view of what’s around you. Prius Prime’s available Panoramic View Monitor 12 uses different cameras to display a panoramic view of what’s around the vehicle.

##### All-electric driving range

Prius Prime’s impressive EPA-estimated all-electric driving range of up to 44 miles 1 lets you run on pure electric power for short commutes. When needed, simply plug into a standard outlet or Level 2 charger to help maximize efficiency.

##### Driving modes

Prius Prime features different driving modes to help optimize your drive: EV (Electric Vehicle) Mode, Auto EV/HV Mode and HV (Hybrid Vehicle) Mode. Auto EV/HV Mode intelligently selects between electric and hybrid driving based on demand and driving conditions. HV Mode combines the gas engine and electrical power from the HV battery.

##### 2.0L hybrid engine

On long drives, Prius Prime has you covered. Its efficient 2.0L 4-cylinder hybrid engine has up to an EPA-estimated 52 combined mpg 13 and will continue to power your drives whenever the battery charge runs low.
