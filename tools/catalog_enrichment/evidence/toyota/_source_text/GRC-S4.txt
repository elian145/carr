PERFORMANCE
Fueled by Rally Spirit
With the GR Corolla, the detail-obsessed TOYOTA GAZOO Racing team has engineered a sports car with the performance, handling and functionality worthy of the GR name. Toyota engineers and technicians have sharpened acceleration and control, and have added an 8-Speed AT with ratios honed through rally-driving. It’s a vehicle designed to meet the approval of master driver Akio Toyoda and now, it’s ready for yours.

Power that Thrills
Under the hood a compact yet powerful G16E-GTS turbo charged, direct/port injected three-cylinder engine serves up 300 hp and 295 lb.-ft. of torque for snappy acceleration out of the corners.
Power that Thrills
Under the hood a compact yet powerful G16E-GTS turbo charged, direct/port injected three-cylinder engine serves up 300 hp and 295 lb.-ft. of torque for snappy acceleration out of the corners.
GR-FOUR AWD
Developed for rally, the GR-FOUR All-Wheel-Drive delivers exceptional traction and control. This lightweight powertrain optimizes drive power to each wheel with just a twist of the dial.
SPORT-TUNED SUSPENSION
Up front the MacPherson-type strut design works to deliver all the engine’s power to the road and provide optimum cornering. While the rear suspension uses a double wishbone type multilink system for maximum agility and stability.
AVAILABLE GAZOO RACING AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION
The GR Corolla sport hatchback is available with a new 8-speed GAZOO Racing Direct Automatic Transmission (DAT), optimized for sporty driving by sensing driver behaviour to anticipate the best time to shift gears.

STOPPING POWER

GRMN Corolla Special Edition
The Ultimate in GR Corolla Performance
The new 2026 GRMN Corolla has arrived, with increased engine torque and chassis enhancements, dedicated aerodynamic and suspension components, and all-wheel-drive control optimized through extensive Nürburgring development. Drivers will love it for its performance, and elevated design cues like full bucket seats, GRMN-themed Brin Naub *R interior, and exclusive colours like Matte Black and Masters Khaki.
DESIGN
Designed by Drivers for Drivers
​Developed in collaboration with the TOYOTA GAZOO Racing World Rally Team and honed by WRC drivers, GR Corolla sport hatchback is designed to perform. Three distinctive grades, Core, Premium MT and Premium AT, focus on lightweight aerodynamics to maximize the performance potential, without compromising strength and safety. Inside, the driver’s cockpit puts the car’s performance within easy reach with details that are just as easy on the eyes.
Hot Hatch Style
Fun to drive. Easy to love. No wonder Canadians love the hot hatch. The GR Corolla performance hatchback injects everyone’s favourite with unique attention-grabbing details. A black GR badged matrix grill leads your gaze over the distinctive hood and tapered roof toward the rear. Here, the wide track and flaring of the rear wings emphasize the car’s low, planted silhouette that’s ready to pounce.
Grounded in High-Performance
For performance that is sure to put a grin on your face, the GR Corolla has all the performance features you crave. Additional torque, brake cooling ducts, front and rear limited slip differential and all the horsepower you need. With 18” gloss black alloy wheels anchoring the core model, and 18” matte black alloy wheels on the Premium grades, and wide tread Michelin Pilot Sport 4 tires that ensure both performance and stability, the GR Corolla has the performance to own the street.

Race-Inspired Interior
Using input from professional rally drivers, the cockpit design puts the car’s performance in reach thanks to a shift lever with short shift strokes and a pull type mechanical parking brake. A brightly lit display and interior trim details around the console, and door handles put the form into function.
Rally-Worthy Instrumentation
Set your sights on a 12.3” Multi-Information Display inspired by the feedback of rally drivers. In any condition, you’ll get a clear view of the gear shift position, tachometer, turbo pressure, as well as the GR meter with AWD mode, without losing sight of the road ahead.
Settle into Sport Seating
Let the standard heated sport seats embrace you the moment you get in. In the Core Edition, these are finished in GR Sport Fabric and in the Premium grades with GR Sport Suede.

TECHNOLOGY
Cutting-Edge Technology
From the Toyota Multimedia System to Toyota Safety Sense 3.0, GR Corolla surrounds you with some of our most advanced tech yet. Enhanced connectivity and convenience features let you do more and see more using intuitive voice and touch controls. The 8” touchscreen display and 6-speaker audio system – 8-speaker JBL Premium Audio on the Premium grades – let you navigate through your music, contacts and more, while you navigate the streets.

Toyota Multimedia System

SAFETY
Powerful Peace of Mind
Matching the exceptional performance is an enhanced Toyota Safety SenseTM 3.0, our most advanced suite of active safety features yet. Take peace of mind to an even greater level with Pre-Collision System to help to support your awareness, especially at intersections.
Learn More About Toyota Safety Sense™ 3.0

Blind Spot Monitor 5 with Rear Cross Traffic Alert 6
For added safety and peace of mind, GR Corolla comes standard with a Blind Spot Monitor (BSM) with Rear Cross Traffic Alert (RCTA). BSM alerts you to the presence of vehicles in adjacent lanes that may have escaped visibility in your side-view mirrors. While RCTA warns you about cross-traffic you can’t see while backing up.

8 Intersection Turning Assist System
The Intersection Turning Assist system is designed to detect a pedestrian or approaching vehicle at intersections under certain conditions, determine if impact is imminent and help reduce impact speed. It is not a substitute for safe and attentive driving. System effectiveness depends on many factors, such as speed, type of turning maneuver, intersection layout, size and position of pedestrian and weather, light and road conditions. See Owner’s Manual for additional limitations and details.
9 Dynamic Radar Cruise Control (DRCC) is designed to assist the driver and is not a substitute for safe and attentive driving practices. System effectiveness is dependent on many factors including road, weather and traffic conditions. Vehicles with manual transmission have regular DRCC. See Owner’s Manual for additional limitations and details.
10 Lane Departure Alert with Steering Assist is designed to read visible lane markers under certain conditions. It provides a visual and audible alert and slight steering force when lane departure is detected. It is not a collision-avoidance system or a substitute for safe and attentive driving. Effectiveness is dependent on many factors including road, weather and vehicle conditions. See Owner’s Manual for additional limitations and details.
11 The Lane Tracing Assist lane centering function is designed to read visible lane markers and detect other vehicles under certain conditions. It is only operational when DRCC is engaged. Not available on vehicles with manual transmissions. See Owner’s Manual for additional limitations and details.
12 Automatic High Beams operate at speeds above 34 km/h. Factors such as a dirty windshield, weather, lighting and terrain limit effectiveness, requiring the driver to manually operate the high beams. See Owner’s Manual for additional limitations and details.
