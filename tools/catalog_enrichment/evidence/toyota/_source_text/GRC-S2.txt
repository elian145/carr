HANDLING
Unleash conﬁdence in every
corner.
GR-FO UR All-W heel Drive
Developed to handle a variety of driving conditions, the GR-FOUR AWD
system features three selectable power settings: Choose to split front
and rear torque distribution to 60:40 for everyday driving, 50:50 for
track and maximum stability, or 30:70 for more torque to the rear
wheels.
Enhanced Handling
With rear geometry that digs in deep and rebound springs dialed to
help keep your wheels down in the corners, GR Corolla’s calibrated
suspension boosts traction and stability, so you can fully enjoy its hair-
raising power. Boost your traction even more with front and rear Torsen®
8 limited-slip diﬀerentials, for dynamic handling.
GR Circuit-Tuned Suspension
Up front, the MacPherson strut suspension, with its circuit-tuned coil
springs, shock absorbers and stabilizer bars, enhances power delivery
and cornering capabilities. In the rear, the double-wishbone multi-link
system maximizes AWD grip and performance.
High-P erform ance Brakes
Save the heat for burning up the track. GR Corolla's ventilated and
slotted brake rotors, with red-painted four-piston front ﬁxed calipers
and two-piston rear calipers, help dissipate heat for better brake
performance, while a mechanical hand-lever brake helps you lock up
the rear.
