LAND CRUISER 2026 Frequently Asked Questions
How much horsepower does a Land Cruiser have?
The Land Cruiser comes with a twin-turbocharged V6, delivering 409 hp and 650 Nm of torque. This Land Cruiser horsepower V6 setup ensures top performance and confident control on every terrain.
Is the Land Cruiser a V6 or V8?
The Toyota LC features a twin-turbocharged 3.5L V6 engine, replacing the previous V8. This torque-rich powertrain delivers top-tier performance and improved power efficiency.
How long will a Land Cruiser last?
With frequent diagnostics and disciplined care, the Toyota Land Cruiser is built to last over 300,000 km. Its formidable durability and dependable drivetrain ensure long-term performance. To keep it running at its best, don't forget to book a service at your nearest Toyota center.
