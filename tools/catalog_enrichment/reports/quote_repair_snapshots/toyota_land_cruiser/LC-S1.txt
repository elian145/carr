Heritage-Inspired Design
Heritage-Inspired Design
Land Cruiser stays true to its roots with a bold, heritage-inspired design built for modern exploration. Featuring a classic “TOYOTA” heritage grille, squared-off side mirrors, and athletic fender flares, this icon is reimagined for today’s adventures—whether tackling rugged trails or roaming city streets with confidence.

Classic Style Cues
Classic Style Cues
Rugged elegance in two distinct trims. Land Cruiser 1958 is a back-to-basics homage to the original FJ25, with iconic round headlights and fabric seats. Land Cruiser trim elevates the ride further with more adventure-ready tech, leather-trimmed seating and rectangular headlights inspired by the FJ62.

Rigid Industries® Color-Selectable LED Fog Lights
Rigid Industries® Color-Selectable LED Fog Lights
Land Cruiser trim’s dual-hue LED fog lights bring more light to your adventures. Effortlessly switch between a crisp white light for low-light driving scenarios or a dynamic yellow beam that enhances contrast in denser conditions.
INTERIOR
Conquer every mile in comfort.
Land Cruiser’s thoughtfully designed interior is ready to elevate every drive. With features like front-seat and steering wheel warmers, available heated side mirrors and available premium leather-trimmed seating, comfort takes the lead on every journey.
PERFORMANCE & CAPABILITY
More grit. More glory.
Full-Time 4WD
Land Cruiser’s full-time 4-wheel-drive system provides power continuously to the front and rear axles to improve traction and stability on various surfaces. An electronically controlled 2-speed transfer case allows you to select the right amount of torque for your driving conditions.
i-FORCE MAX Hybrid Powertrain
Land Cruiser's powertrain combines hybrid tech with an impressive turbo 4-cylinder hybrid engine to generate an electrifying 326 net combined horsepower with 465 lb.-ft. of torque.
Towing Capacity
Bring more along for the ride. With a tremendous towing capacity of 6000 lbs., you’ll be able to tackle the trails with whatever you need to take your trek to the next level.
Drive Modes
Three distinct drive modes let you instantly tailor your drive with the touch of a button. Sport Mode indulges your spirited side with the feeling of sharper acceleration, while Eco Mode adjusts throttle input to help maximize your efficiency. Normal Mode blends fuel economy with performance.
Rear and Center Locking Differentials
More off-road; more control. Engage Land Cruiser’s electronically locking differentials with the press of a button to distribute available torque for better traction while maneuvering through challenging terrain.
Stabilizer Disconnect Mechanism
While Land Cruiser’s frame provides stability to support your everyday drives, sometimes off-road excursions require something different. Activate Land Cruiser’s available Stabilizer Disconnect Mechanism (SDM) for greater wheel articulation as you navigate uneven trails and rocky terrain.
Power Inverter
Explore the wilderness without limitations. Land Cruiser’s 2400W power inverter integrates the ability to charge and operate your compatible essentials wherever your off-road journey takes you. From laptops to coffee makers, this high-capacity outlet helps you power up your adventures.
Multi-Terrain Select With Crawl Control
Conquer with confidence. Land Cruiser’s available Multi-Terrain Select (MTS) helps you navigate varying landscapes with improved traction at different speeds. Crawl Control (CRAWL) helps take on difficult terrain by automatically modulating the throttle and brakes, so you can focus on steering.

Multimedia Screen
Multimedia Screen
Land Cruiser’s standard 8-in. or available 12.3-in. Toyota Audio Multimedia touchscreen heightens your experience through sight, touch and voice. With an active trial or subscription, the voice recognition of Toyota’s Intelligent Assistant lets you use simple phrases like “Hey, Toyota” to find destinations, stream music and more—keeping you effortlessly connected to every adventure.
Console Cool Box
Console Cool Box
Land Cruiser's available center-console cool box recirculates air from your air conditioner to help keep your beverages chilled while you explore remote destinations or cruise down city streets.
Qi Wireless Charger and USB-C Ports
Qi Wireless Charger and USB-C Ports
Charge ahead. With Land Cruiser’s available Qi-compatible wireless charger, you can simply place your Qi-compatible device to wirelessly charge up while on the go. And for more versatility and added convenience, Land Cruiser comes with three USB-C fast-charging ports so you can connect to and power up a variety of devices.
Heated/Ventilated Front Seats
Heated/Ventilated Front Seats
Fine-tune your comfort zone in no time. Standard heated front seats help warm up every drive, while the available Premium Package offers heated and ventilated seats to the front and rear outboard seats—making every journey more inviting.
Head-Up Display
Head-Up Display
Get a better view of where you’re going. The available full-color Head-Up Display (HUD) projects your speed, select safety alerts and other customized driving info right onto the windshield so you can focus on your drive.
60/40 Split Rear Seat
60/40 Split Rear Seat
From commutes to campfires, Land Cruiser’s 60/40 rear bench configuration balances everyday functionality with outdoor adventures. Fold down either side or both to enjoy ample room for passengers and flexible cargo space that adapts to whatever your adventure calls for.

No Cost Maintenance Plan and Roadside Assistance

Blind Spot Monitor With Rear Cross-Traffic Alert
Blind Spot Monitor With Rear Cross-Traffic Alert
Standard Blind Spot Monitor (BSM) is designed to warn you when a vehicle in the next lane enters Land Cruiser’s blind spot on either side of the vehicle. And when you’re in reverse, Rear Cross-Traffic Alert (RCTA) is designed to help detect vehicles approaching from either side, warning you with side mirror indicators and a warning tone.
Integrated Backup Camera
Integrated Backup Camera
Put Land Cruiser in reverse and the standard backup camera automatically activates to show you what’s behind your vehicle on the available 12.3-in. multimedia display.
Front Cross-Traffic Alert
Front Cross-Traffic Alert
Available Front Cross-Traffic Alert (FCTA) is designed to alert you to vehicles approaching the front end of your vehicle. A warning and buzzer are indicated on the Multi-Information Display (MID), and the direction of approach is shown on the available Head-Up Display (HUD), to help give you better awareness of your surroundings.
Multi-Terrain Monitor
Multi-Terrain Monitor
When venturing through uncharted territory, Land Cruiser’s available Multi-Terrain Monitor (MTM) is designed to help you check your immediate surroundings for potential obstacles, even on the terrain beneath your vehicle. Simply press a button to display front-, rear- and side-camera views on the available 12.3-in. touchscreen.
