Delete your CarNet account

Google Play and App Store require a way to request account deletion on the web as well as in the app.

Fastest: delete in the app

  1. Open CarNet and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account.
  3. Confirm with the SMS code sent to your registered phone.

This permanently deletes your account and associated data (listings, messages, favorites, saved searches) when possible. See our Privacy Policy.

Or request deletion here

If you cannot use the app, submit this form. We will process verified requests and remove your account and personal data. You may need to confirm ownership of the phone or email you provide.

Support: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Typical processing time for web requests is within a few business days after we verify ownership. In-app deletion is immediate after SMS confirmation.
