  • Cloud hosting and API infrastructure (production API hosting)
  • Managed PostgreSQL database hosting

  • Email and SMS providers (verification codes, password reset, support, account-deletion confirmation)
  • Attribution / deep-link providers such as Airbridge when configured for install attribution or shared listing links

6. Retention

We retain information while your account is active and as needed to operate the Service, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. When you delete your account, we permanently delete your account and associated data such as listings, messages, favorites, and saved searches when possible. If technical constraints prevent a full hard delete, we deactivate the account, anonymize personal identifiers, and scrub listing media and private fields so they are no longer publicly attributable to you. Some security, backup, or legal records (for example rate-limit or audit logs) may be retained for a limited period.

7. Security

Production API traffic uses HTTPS (encryption in transit). Passwords are stored using strong one-way hashing on the server. Auth tokens on your device should be protected by your device lock. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; use a strong, unique password where you set one, and do not share SMS codes.
